Linen Prints

Fabloom's linen print collection is among our most celebrated offerings. We combine the natural, breathable quality of pure linen with the artistry of both traditional block printing and modern digital printing to create fabrics that are truly extraordinary.

Our block-print linen uses hand-carved wooden blocks and mineral dyes — a process unchanged for centuries. Our digital linen prints offer photographic precision and an unlimited colour gamut. Both processes deliver vibrant, wash-fast results on premium linen bases.
