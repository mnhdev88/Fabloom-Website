Areas We Supply

Silk & Linen Fabric Supplier Across South India

Home / Areas We Supply

Fabloom is a silk and linen manufacturer in Bhagalpur, Bihar. We weave, dye and finish at our own unit and despatch across India. We do not operate shops, showrooms or looms anywhere in South India — what follows is a list of the towns we regularly supply, not a list of branches.

Each page below explains who we typically supply in that town, which of our fabrics tend to suit that market, and how despatch works. Where a town has its own weaving tradition, we say so rather than pretending our cloth is local to it.

If your town is not listed, we can still supply it — these are simply the markets we hear from most often. Send an enquiry and we will quote for delivery wherever you are.
